Her Last Check-In Was at a Beachfront Café

December 14, 2026 · 3 min read

Yuki is a digital nomad spending six months in Bali, working from a local coworking space by day. Her parents back in California video-call once or twice a month. Alongside her parents, she added Tom—a friend she met at the coworking space—as a Carer.

She made a habit of checking in every evening after work and turning on location sharing whenever she was out.

That afternoon, she checked in at her favorite beachfront café, left her bag behind the counter, and went for a swim. The surf was rougher than usual, and a rip current pulled her out. A surf instructor managed to pull her back to shore, though she had swallowed a lot of water and was rushed to a nearby clinic—leaving her phone and bag back at the café.

That evening, she missed her check-in. Tom got the alert and called with no answer. Tapping her afternoon check-in, he saw the pin right at that beachfront café. He rode his scooter over, where staff told him her bag was still there and that a foreign woman had been taken to the local clinic earlier.

Tom tracked down the clinic, stayed with her until late that night, and texted her parents in California: "She's safe, and I'm right here with her."

"My parents are thousands of miles away," Yuki said, "but my last check-in told the closest person where to find me."

When living abroad alone, add a local friend as a Carer alongside your family.
