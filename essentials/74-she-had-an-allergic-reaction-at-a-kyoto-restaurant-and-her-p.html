She Had an Allergic Reaction at a Kyoto Restaurant, and Her Phone Was at the Hostel

December 23, 2026 · 1 min read

Rachel went on a five-day solo trip to Kyoto. Before leaving, she sent the name of her hostel to her best friend Megan in Chicago, and they agreed that Rachel would check in on ANLY every night when she got back to her room. They usually only caught up every week or two.

On her third night, she went out for dinner, taking only her wallet and leaving her phone at the hostel to charge. The dish she ordered contained shrimp paste, which she was allergic to. After just a few bites, she started having trouble breathing. The restaurant staff called an ambulance and rushed her to the hospital.

That night, she missed her check-in. Megan received the alert, and when her texts went unanswered, she remembered the name of the hostel and gave them a call. The front desk said Rachel had gone out for the evening and hadn't returned yet, but they promised to help call nearby hospitals.

An hour later, the receptionist called Megan back: Rachel was at the hospital and her condition had stabilized. Megan was able to speak with Rachel through the hospital's phone and then helped notify her family back in the US.

"I just went out to grab a quick dinner," Rachel said. "I never expected that one meal would completely cut me off from everyone for the entire night."

When traveling alone, share your accommodation details with a friend. If you miss a check-in, they'll know exactly where to start looking.
