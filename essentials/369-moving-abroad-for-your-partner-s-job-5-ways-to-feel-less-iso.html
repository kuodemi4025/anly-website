Moving Abroad for Your Partner's Job? 5 Ways to Feel Less Isolated

December 7, 2026 · 3 min read

While the relocated partner heads to an office every day with built-in coworkers and structure, the trailing spouse is often the one left home alone—navigating an unfamiliar language without local friends yet.

  1. Build a steady weekly routine
    Sign up for a local language class, join a fitness studio, or volunteer in the community. Having places to be gives your week rhythm.
  2. Find local expat or hometown communities
    Most major cities have expat spouse groups, international clubs, or online meetups. The first gathering can feel a little awkward, but showing up changes everything.
  3. Share how you're feeling with your partner
    Saying, "You're busy all day while I'm just waiting for you to come home," opens the door for both of you to figure things out together.
  4. Don't rely solely on your partner to navigate local life
    Save local emergency numbers, nearby clinics, and your building manager's contact on your own phone so you feel confident even when your partner travels for work.
  5. Keep family back home reassured
    Add your parents, siblings, close friends, and partner as Carers on ANLY and check in daily. Even on days when your partner is away on a business trip, several people know you're safe—and get alerted if you miss a check-in.

Moving abroad for love is a bold leap; your everyday well-being deserves care too.
